Stay current with Colorado leave laws

Colorado's leave requirements can be complex, especially when state and federal laws overlap. Knowing how these regulations work together is essential for professionals responsible for managing employee leave.
DMEC's Colorado State Leave Laws Microcredential helps you stay current with state requirements and expand your knowledge of the regulations that affect your work.
You'll explore:
  • Colorado's Family and Medical Leave Insurance (FAMLI) program.
  • Paid sick leave under the Healthy Families and Workplaces Act (HFWA).
  • Workers' compensation and other protected leave.
  • How Colorado's leave laws interact with federal requirements.
