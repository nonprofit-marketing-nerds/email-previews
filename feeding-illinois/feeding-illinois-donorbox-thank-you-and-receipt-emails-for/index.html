2. Hunters Feeding Illinois receipt

Subject: Thank you for supporting Hunters Feeding Illinois (receipt inside)

FEEDINGILLINOIS

Dear Jane,

Thank you for your gift to Hunters Feeding Illinois, part of Feeding Illinois’ Farm to Food Bank program.

Hunters donate their deer. Your gift helps pay local meat processors to turn the deer into ground venison for food banks, and Feeding Illinois matches every dollar. So a $50 gift becomes $100, enough to cover the processing of one deer.

Between July 2025 and June 2026, hunters donated 1,270 deer, and 23 processors turned them into 47,050 pounds of venison. That’s about 141,150 servings. Meat is what food pantries and their guests want most, and it costs the most per pound. You just helped with that.

3. Grain for Good receipt

Subject: Thank you for supporting Grain for Good (receipt inside)

FEEDINGILLINOIS

Dear Jane,

Thank you for your gift to Grain for Good.

Grain for Good is a Feeding Illinois program that started with a simple ask of Illinois grain farmers: when you sell at the elevator, donate the value of some of that grain. That money buys ground pork from Illinois hog farmers, processed at local meat lockers and delivered to nearby food pantries.

In its early 2025 pilot, it put 3,000 pounds of ground pork on the shelves of the Open Arms Food Pantry in Metamora. Your gift helps keep it going, so more Illinois pork reaches Illinois neighbors.
